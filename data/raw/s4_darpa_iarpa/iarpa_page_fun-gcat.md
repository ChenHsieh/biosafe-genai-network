## Program: Functional Genomic and Computational Assessment of Threats (Fun GCAT)
## Duration: June 2017 – September 2022
## BAA: IARPA-BAA-16-08 (Closed November 8, 2016)

## Core Mission
Develop rapid assessment methods for DNA sequences to identify biological threats.
Enable improved detection of bio-error or bio-terror.

## Key Achievements
- AI-driven threat screening software replacing traditional lookup-table methods
- Computational pipelines for: organism origin, biological functions, and danger level
- 500x improvement in computational efficiency over state-of-the-art
- Stable performance on short sequences (<50 base pairs)
- Optical, cell-based tests for identifying immune system disruptors
- Discovery of new functions in dozens of virus genes
