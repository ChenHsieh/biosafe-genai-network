## Program: Personalized Protective Biosystem (PPB)
## Publication Date: April 19, 2021
## Program Manager: Eric Van Gieson

## Awarded Performers
- FLIR Systems
- Leidos
- Charles River Analytics

## Program Focus
Lightweight materials and tissue-protective countermeasures against chemical and biological threats.

## Duration: Five years (two technical areas)
- TA1: External protection using smart, lightweight materials
- TA2: Internal threat neutralization at vulnerable tissue barriers
