# DARPA PREEMPT News Release
# Source: https://www.darpa.mil/news/2019/medical-preparedness
# Fetched: 2026-03-12 (via WebFetch)

## Program: PREventing EMerging Pathogenic Threats (PREEMPT)
## Duration: 3.5 years (announced January 2018)
## Program Manager: Dr. Brad Ringeisen

## Lead Organizations & Principal Investigators

### 1. Autonomous Therapeutics, Inc.
- PI: Dr. Ariel Weinberger
- Focus: Airborne HPAI in birds/small mammals; tick-borne CCHF
- Sub-teams: CSIRO Australian Animal Health Lab, Navy Medical Research Unit-2, UCLA, U of Chicago Medical School, UT Medical Branch

### 2. University of California, Davis
- PIs: Dr. Peter Barry and Dr. Brian Bird
- Focus: Lassa virus spillover from rodents; Ebola virus in rhesus macaques
- Sub-teams: Leibniz Institute for Experimental Virology, Mount Sinai School of Medicine, NIH Rocky Mountain Labs, The Vaccine Group Ltd., U of Glasgow, U of Idaho, U of Western Australia

### 3. Institut Pasteur
- PI: Dr. Carla Saleh
- Focus: Mosquito-borne arboviruses
- Sub-teams: Institut Pasteur International Network (Cambodia, CAR, France, French Guiana, Madagascar, Uruguay), Latham BioPharm Group, Virginia Tech

### 4. Montana State University
- PI: Dr. Raina Plowright
- Focus: Henipavirus spillover from bats
- Sub-teams: Cary Institute, Colorado State, Cornell, Griffith U, Johns Hopkins, NIH Rocky Mountain Labs, Penn State, Texas Tech, UC Berkeley, UCLA, U of Cambridge

### 5. The Pirbright Institute
- PI: Dr. Luke Alphey
- Focus: Mosquito transmission of flaviviruses (Dengue, West Nile, Zika)
- Sub-teams: U of Nottingham, U of Tartu
