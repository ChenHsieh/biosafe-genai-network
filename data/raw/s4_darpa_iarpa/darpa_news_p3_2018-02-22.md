# DARPA P3 (Pandemic Prevention Platform) News Release
# Source: https://www.darpa.mil/news/2018/halt-outbreaks
# Fetched: 2026-03-12 (via WebFetch)

## Date: February 22, 2018
## Program Manager: Col. Matthew Hepburn, M.D.
## Announcement Venue: AAAS Annual Meeting in Austin, Texas

## Performer Institutions
- Duke University
- Vanderbilt University
- MedImmune
- AbCellera Biologics Inc.

## Program Goals
End-to-end pandemic response platform capable of halting infectious disease outbreaks within 60 days from initial pathogen sample collection.

## Key Objectives
- Discover and optimize antibodies within initial outbreak weeks
- Deliver antibodies using nucleic acid technology (DNA/RNA-based)
- Achieve protective antibody serum concentrations within three days of administration

## Target Pathogens
Influenza, chikungunya, MERS-CoV, and Mayaro virus.
