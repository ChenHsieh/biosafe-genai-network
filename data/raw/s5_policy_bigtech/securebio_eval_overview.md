## Evaluation Types and Benchmarks

### Virology Capabilities Test (VCT)
First-of-its-kind benchmark measuring model's ability to provide expert-level
practical assistance in work with viruses. Covered by Time magazine.

### Claude 4 CBRN Risk Assessment included:
- DNA synthesis screening evasion task set for LLM agents
- Creative biology scenarios as proxies for novel biology abilities
- Long-form virology tasks (co-developed with Deloitte and Signature Science)
- End-to-end pathogen acquisition testing
