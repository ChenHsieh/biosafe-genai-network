## Study 1: Red Team Exercise (2024)
- Title: "The Operational Risks of AI in Large-Scale Biological Attacks"
- Teams role-played as malign nonstate actors planning biological attacks
- Some teams had LLM access, others only internet
- Finding: No statistically significant difference in plan viability with/without LLMs
- Conclusion: "biological weapon attack planning currently lies beyond the capability frontier of LLMs"

## Study 2: Press Release (Jan 25, 2024)
- Title: "Current Artificial Intelligence Does Not Meaningfully Increase Risk of a Biological Weapons Attack"
- Summary of red team study findings

## Study 3: Automated Grading (2025)
- Title: "Automated Grading for Efficiently Evaluating the Dual-Use Biological Capabilities of LLMs"
- Proof-of-concept automated grader for LLM bio capabilities
- Assessed LLMs on knowledge-based questions and lab protocol generation
- Covered 30+ models

## Relationship to OpenAI
- OpenAI's own 2024 biosecurity study (100 participants, GPT-4) parallels RAND's methodology
- Gryphon Scientific developed the evaluation framework for OpenAI's study
- RAND and OpenAI studies both used uplift methodology
