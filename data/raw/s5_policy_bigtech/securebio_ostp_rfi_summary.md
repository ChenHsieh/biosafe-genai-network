# SecureBio RFI Submission to OSTP — AI Action Plan
# Source: https://files.nitrd.gov/90-fr-9088/SecureBio-AI-RFI-2025.pdf
# Date: March 15, 2025
# Saved: securebio_ostp_rfi_2025-03-15.pdf

## Submitter: SecureBio, Inc.
## Submitted to: Office of Science and Technology Policy (OSTP) and NITRD National Coordination Office

## Key Claims
- SecureBio's AIxBio team focuses on AI capabilities research and evaluations
- They "collaborate with government agencies and most of the major LLM developers"
- "Leading AI models now exceed the capabilities of human experts in providing practical guidance in bioweapon-relevant work"
- Expect capabilities to arrive enabling novel/augmented bioweapons by 2026 or 2027

## Recommendations
1. Empower US AISI (in NIST) to continue AI evaluation work
2. Direct US AISI to create recommended standards for AI evaluations
3. USG evaluations should identify models with strong bio-capabilities
4. Establish biosecurity-focused AI governance for high-priority models
5. Classify key AI capabilities as dual-use technologies (like physical bio materials)
6. Incentivize KYC verification for AI systems capable of expert-level bioweapon guidance
7. Fund specialized red-teaming focused on bioweapon capabilities
8. Lead development of international standards
