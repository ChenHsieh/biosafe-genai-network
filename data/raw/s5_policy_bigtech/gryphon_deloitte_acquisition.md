## Deloitte Acquisition (April 2024)
- Deloitte acquired substantially all assets of Gryphon Scientific LLC
- Gryphon now operates as part of Deloitte
- Expands Deloitte's security, science, and public health capabilities

## OpenAI Biosecurity Study
- Title: "Building an early warning system for LLM-aided biological threat creation"
- OpenAI authors: Tejal Patwardhan, Kevin Liu, Todor Markov + team
- Gryphon authors: Rocco Casagrande, Froggi Jackson + team
- 100 participants: 50 PhD bio experts + 50 students
- Result: GPT-4 "at most mildly useful" for bio threat creation
- Published: January 2024
