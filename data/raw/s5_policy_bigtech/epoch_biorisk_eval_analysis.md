## AI Labs Conducting Evaluations

### Anthropic
- Most comprehensive: 10+ biorisk assessments for Claude Opus 4
- Methods: Multiple-choice benchmarks, uplift trials, red teaming, manual grading
- Activated AI Safety Level 3 (ASL-3) protections based on results

### OpenAI
- Evaluated o3 and o4-mini models (4 described assessments)
- Methods: Autograded and manually graded evaluations, historical uplift trials with GPT-4

### Google DeepMind
- Evaluated Gemini 2.5 Pro (5 total biorisk assessments)
- Methods: Primarily multiple-choice autograded questions + one manual evaluation

### Meta
- Limited disclosure on Llama 4 models
- Methods: CBRNE evaluations including red teaming (minimal transparency)

### xAI, Mistral, Alibaba, DeepSeek
- No publicly released biosecurity evaluation info identified

## Third-Party Evaluation Organizations
1. SecureBio — Virology Capabilities Test (VCT), red-teaming
2. FutureHouse — Language Agent Biology Benchmark (LAB-Bench)
3. UK AI Security Institute (AISI) — Evaluated Claude 3.5 Sonnet, o1 (May 2024)
4. RAND Corporation — 3 separate biorisk analyses covering 30+ models
5. NIST CAISI — Mentioned as potential evaluator
6. Deloitte — Co-developed long-form virology tasks with SecureBio
7. Signature Science — Co-developed virology tasks with SecureBio

## Evaluation Methods
- Benchmarks (40-80% of evaluations): VCT, LAB-Bench, WMDP, GPQA-Bio, LitQA2
- Uplift trials: Text-based performance improvements with AI assistance
- Red teaming: Adversarial testing
- Manual grading: Expert-assessed open-ended responses
- Human baselines: Expert virologist performance comparison
